# CESIM-Capstone-Strategic-Business-Analysis
Capstone project on strategic business and financial analysis across six simulation rounds.

### Capstone Project | Orange Team | IIIT Lucknow | 2026

## Project Overview

This capstone project focuses on strategic business and financial analysis through a six-round business simulation. The project examines how changing industry conditions influence market opportunities, competitive pressure, pricing decisions, operational efficiency, and financial performance.

The analysis tracks the industry's transition from initial market expansion to mature-market stabilization, highlighting the importance of strategic decision-making, financial discipline, and operational balance.

## Project Objectives

- Analyse industry conditions and changing market dynamics across six simulation rounds.
- Understand the impact of demand acceleration and competitive pressure on business strategy.
- Examine the importance of pricing decisions, operational efficiency, and financial discipline.
- Evaluate business performance using key financial indicators.
- Identify strategic implications for long-term profitability and financial sustainability.

## Industry Analysis: Six Simulation Rounds

| Round | Industry Situation | Key Strategic Implication |
|---|---|---|
| Round 1 | Initial market expansion | Opportunity to capture early market share |
| Round 2 | Demand acceleration | Technology launch supported diversification |
| Round 3 | Increased competitive pressure | Pricing and efficiency became critical |
| Round 4 | Market leadership formation | Leading firms consolidated their positions |
| Round 5 | Overcapacity and profitability crisis | Financial discipline became essential |
| Round 6 | Mature-market stabilization | Recovery and operational balance restored |

## Key Financial Performance Indicators

| Metric | Reported Result |
|---|---:|
| Simulation Rounds | 6 |
| Best Earnings Per Share (EPS) | 16.40 |
| Peak Return on Equity (ROE) | 37.28% |
| Final Revenue | 1.63M |


## Key Insights

- **Market Dynamics:** Industry conditions evolved from market expansion and rising demand to stronger competition and market stabilization.
- **Competitive Strategy:** Pricing decisions and operational efficiency became increasingly important as competitive pressure intensified.
- **Financial Discipline:** The overcapacity and profitability challenges in Round 5 highlighted the importance of disciplined business decisions.
- **Operational Sustainability:** The final round emphasized recovery, operational balance, and long-term financial sustainability.

## Conclusion

The simulation demonstrates how business performance depends on adapting strategies to changing industry conditions. It highlights the relationship between competitive positioning, operational efficiency, financial discipline, and sustainable business performance.
